## Quick answer

LiveChat is a real-time customer support chat platform built around a website chat widget, an AI
Copilot assistant, a chatbot, knowledge-base tools, and a help desk, tied together with 200+
integrations across CRM, ecommerce, and marketing tools. Pricing is per-agent/month across four

## What LiveChat actually does

LiveChat is built around real-time chat between a business and its website visitors, with features
consistently described across sources: message sneak-peek (seeing what a visitor is typing before
they send it), chat transfer between agents, proactive outreach to visitors browsing the site,
routing chats to specific agents by page or topic, and an asynchronous mode so a conversation can
continue over chat and email even when one side is offline.

Beyond the chat widget itself, LiveChat markets a broader support bundle: a chatbot, a knowledge
management tool, and a help desk, plus an AI Copilot that helps agents rewrite messages and
summarize conversations. All tiers reportedly include the Copilot assistant, canned responses, and
web/mobile/desktop apps.

### Integrations

Sources consistently describe a marketplace of 200+ integrations spanning ecommerce, CRM,
marketing, analytics, and automation tools — positioning LiveChat as something that plugs into an
existing sales/support stack rather than requiring you to migrate everything to it.

## Who this is a good fit for

Based on the feature set and pricing structure (not hands-on long-term testing):
- Sales-driven businesses where live chat is a conversion tool, not just a support ticket queue —
  reviews consistently position LiveChat as strong for engagement/conversion use cases
- Support teams that want chat, a chatbot, and a knowledge base under one login instead of
  separately purchased tools
- Businesses with an existing CRM/ecommerce/marketing stack that need a chat tool with broad
  integration coverage rather than a standalone widget

## Who should NOT buy this

- Very small or pre-revenue sites where a simpler, cheaper (or free-tier) chat widget would cover
  the need — the Business tier's advanced routing/analytics is overkill until you have a real
  multi-agent support queue.
- Teams that need certainty on price before evaluating — given the discrepancy we found across
  sources, budget-sensitive buyers should get a quote/confirm current pricing directly rather than
  planning around any single number found online, including the ranges in this article.
- Anyone expecting the AI Copilot to fully replace human agents — it's described across sources as
  an assist/summarize tool for agents, not an autonomous support agent.
