## Quick answer

Systeme.io is an all-in-one online business platform: sales funnels, email marketing, course
hosting, a website/blog builder, affiliate program management, and marketing automation, bundled
into a single dashboard instead of six separate subscriptions. It has a genuinely **free permanent
plan** ($0, no credit card, no time limit), then three paid tiers: **Startup at $17/mo**, **Webinar

## Who this is a good fit for

Based on the feature set and pricing structure (not hands-on long-term testing):
- Solo creators and small businesses that currently pay for 3+ separate tools (funnel builder,
  email tool, course host) and want to consolidate into one lower-cost subscription
- Anyone testing a new digital-product idea who wants to start on a genuinely free plan before
  committing to a paid tier
- Businesses that want their affiliate program managed on the same platform as the funnel and
  emails driving it, rather than a separate affiliate-tracking tool

## Who should NOT buy this

- Businesses that need deep, granular analytics or heavy third-party integrations — multiple
  independent reviews consistently note Systeme.io's analytics and customization are more basic
  than dedicated, higher-priced tools built for one job at a time.
- Teams already deep in a specific ecosystem (e.g., a mature Kajabi course catalog or a complex
  ClickFunnels setup) — migration cost may outweigh the savings from consolidating tools.
- Anyone expecting enterprise-grade design flexibility — reviews describe the funnel/page builder
  as fast and beginner-friendly, not as customizable as dedicated page builders.

## FAQ

**Is the free plan actually free forever?**
Every source we checked describes it as a permanent free tier (2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1
course, 1 automation rule), not a time-limited trial, and no credit card is required to start.

**What's the difference between Startup, Webinar, and Unlimited?**
Startup adds unlimited email and funnel automations over the free tier. Webinar adds webinar
hosting and higher limits. Unlimited raises the resource limits further and unlocks the full
automation suite. Exact current caps should be confirmed on Systeme.io's own pricing page.

**Does Systeme.io charge transaction fees?**
Sources describe the free plan as having 0% transaction fees; confirm current fee policy on paid
tiers directly with Systeme.io, since we could not verify a consistent fee figure for every paid
tier across sources.

## Bottom line

Systeme.io's core pitch — six tools bundled at a price far below buying each separately — holds up
