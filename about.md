# How We Review

We're a research site, not a paid testing lab — and we think that's a strength, not a weakness, as
long as we're honest about it. This page explains exactly how our guides are built, what we can
tell you, and what we can't.

---

## What we actually do

For every guide we:

1. **Pull the vendor's live pricing and feature data** — read directly from the tool's own pricing
   page on the date shown at the top of the article.
2. **Compare tools on the same criteria** so pricing tiers and limits can be judged fairly side by
   side.
3. **Summarize what verified users report** — recurring themes across published reviews (G2,
   Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit), not a single cherry-picked quote.
4. **Score each option** on a transparent, weighted system, and show the working.

Everything is dated, and everything is sourced.

---

## What we can — and can't — tell you

**We can** verify, from research: pricing, feature lists, usage limits, and the vendor's own
published claims. These are documented facts, and we check them against the source.

**We can't** tell you how a tool feels in your specific daily workflow over months of use — because
**no one here has used every tool as their primary daily driver.** Where hands-on, long-term fit
matters most to you, use the tool's free trial before committing to a paid plan.

You'll never read the words "I've used this every day for months" on this site unless it's true and
disclosed. We'd rather under-claim than overstate.

---

## How this site makes money

Some links on our guides are **affiliate links**. If you sign up through one, we may earn a
commission at no extra cost to you. That's it — vendors don't pay us for placement or for a good
ranking, and a tool can't buy its way up our scoring.

Every page with affiliate links says so, at the top, before the content.
