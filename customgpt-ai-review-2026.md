## Quick answer

CustomGPT.ai is a no-code platform for building AI chatbots that answer questions using **only**
the content you feed it (your website, help docs, PDFs, Notion pages, etc.) — a technique called
retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). The pitch is fewer hallucinations, because the AI is fenced
in to your own knowledge base and cites its sources. Plans start at **$99/month**, scaling up to a
**$499/month Premium tier** and a custom-priced Enterprise tier.

## What CustomGPT.ai actually does

CustomGPT.ai lets a business upload its own content — a website, a help center, PDFs, Google Drive
or Notion docs — and turns it into a chatbot that only answers from that content, with citations
back to the source document. That's the core differentiator marketed across every source we
checked: it's built specifically to reduce hallucination by restricting the model's answers to a
defined knowledge base, rather than letting it draw on general internet training data.

Common use cases described consistently across sources:
- Customer-support chatbots that answer FAQs from a company's own help docs
- Internal knowledge search tools for employees (pointed at Confluence, SharePoint, Notion, etc.)
- Content generation and e-commerce product Q&A grounded in a store's own catalog

### Integrations

Sources list native connections to Confluence, Google Drive, Notion, SharePoint, Azure AD Connect,
Shopify Collabs, Squarespace, YouTube, Zendesk AI, HubSpot (Content Hub and Marketing Hub), and
Zapier — giving it a fairly wide reach into where a business's existing documentation already
lives, rather than requiring content to be re-typed into the platform.

## Who this is a good fit for

Based on the feature set and pricing tier structure (not on hands-on testing):
- Businesses that already have a large, organized knowledge base (help docs, product catalog,
  internal wiki) and want a chatbot that won't wander off-topic or invent answers
- Teams that need the chatbot wired into tools they already use (Zendesk, HubSpot, Shopify, Notion)
  rather than a standalone widget
- Companies with compliance/accuracy requirements where "the AI made something up" is a real risk —
  the citation-back-to-source design is aimed directly at that problem

## Who should NOT buy this

- Solo creators or very small sites with a thin knowledge base — $99/month is a real cost, and a
  RAG chatbot is only as good as the content you feed it. If you don't have much documentation to
  train it on, you're paying for infrastructure you can't fully use yet.
- Anyone who needs a fully custom conversational agent with logic/branching beyond Q&A over
  documents — CustomGPT's core design is retrieval-and-answer, not a general workflow/automation
  builder (compare something like Bardeen or Stack AI if that's the actual need).
- Teams that haven't budgeted past the Standard tier but expect Premium-level chatbot/content
  limits — the jump from $99 to $499/month is large, so map your real content volume against the
  published limits before committing.

## FAQ

**Is CustomGPT.ai the same as ChatGPT?**
No. It's built on top of large language model infrastructure but restricts answers to content you
provide, rather than the general model's broad training data — that's the whole point of the
product.

**Does it really eliminate hallucinations?**
No tool can guarantee zero hallucinations. What's consistently described across sources is that it
*reduces* the risk by fencing answers to your own content and citing sources — not that it's
impossible for it to ever be wrong.

**Is there a free trial?**
CustomGPT's own materials describe a free trial being available; confirm current trial terms on
their site before signing up, since trial policies change.

**How does pricing compare to building your own RAG chatbot?**
That's outside the scope of this review — it depends heavily on your team's engineering resources
and how much ongoing maintenance you're willing to take on. We're not qualified to state a number
here without doing that build-vs-buy analysis for a specific business.

## Bottom line

CustomGPT.ai's core promise — an AI chatbot that only answers from your own content and shows its
sources — is a real, verifiable design choice, not vague marketing. The pricing is transparent for

already has substantial documentation and wants a support/knowledge chatbot that won't invent
